# Metallicity ([M/H])

[← Parameters](README.md) · [notebook 5 §4](../../notebooks/05_global_parameters.ipynb)

**What it is.** log<sub>10</sub> of the heavy-element fraction relative to the Sun: 0 = solar, −0.5 ≈ ⅓ solar,
+0.5 ≈ 3× solar.

**What it changes.** Fewer heavy elements → less H<sub>2</sub>O, CH<sub>4</sub>, CO, CO<sub>2</sub> absorption, but the
hydrogen (and its collision-induced absorption) is unchanged — so H<sub>2</sub> CIA becomes relatively more important.

**Observed sign of low metallicity** (Rustamkulov et al. 2026, low-metallicity sequence at L0 and L7):
the J (1.3 µm) to K (2.2 µm) peak ratio **reverses**, because H<sub>2</sub> CIA dominates from ~1.7 to 3 µm.

**What can mimic it.** High gravity (also strengthens CIA), temperature, clouds.
